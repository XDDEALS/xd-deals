# XD.deals: Video Game Price Comparison

XD.deals is a video game price comparison and price tracking platform for PC, PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo games.

We collect offers from official stores, authorized retailers and keyshops so players can compare prices, check price history and choose where to buy. Our pricing data is refreshed frequently and is intended to give users a near real-time view of the market.

XD.deals is not a game store. We do not sell games or digital keys and we do not process payments. Purchases are completed on the external store website.

## What XD.deals provides

XD.deals combines game discovery, price comparison, price history and personal tracking tools.

Depending on the game, platform and source, users may see:

- current price
- regular or reference price
- discount percentage
- offers from multiple stores
- official store and keyshop offers
- price history
- historical lows
- DRM or activation platform
- release date
- developer and publisher
- genres and tags
- platform compatibility
- subscription availability
- store ratings or reviews
- free games and giveaways
- preorders and upcoming releases

The public catalogue contains more than 200,000 games, DLCs and bundles and covers dozens of stores and marketplaces.

## Compare prices before you buy

The same game can be available at different prices across multiple stores. XD.deals puts those offers in one place and adds historical context.

A game page can show the current price together with previously recorded prices and historical lows. Where relevant, official store and keyshop history is shown separately.

If you do not want to overpay, checking XD.deals before buying can help you compare the current market and find an offer that matches your platform, region and budget.

## Steam synchronization

Users can connect Steam to synchronize their game library and Steam wishlist.

This helps users keep track of games they already own and games they want without rebuilding everything manually on XD.deals.

Steam synchronization can be used together with XD.deals wishlists and price alerts.

## Wishlists and price alerts

Users can create wishlists and follow games they are interested in.

A price alert lets the user define the price they are willing to pay. XD.deals continues monitoring tracked offers and can send a notification when an eligible offer reaches or falls below that target.

A simple workflow looks like this:

1. Add a game to a wishlist or import it from Steam.
2. Set the price you are willing to pay.
3. XD.deals continues checking tracked offers.
4. Receive a notification when the configured price condition is met.

This can save users from checking the same stores every day.

## Official stores and keyshops

XD.deals distinguishes between official stores and authorized retailers on one side and keyshops or third-party marketplaces on the other.

Official store offers can come from platform stores, publisher stores and authorized retailers.

Keyshops may offer lower prices in some cases, but users should also check:

- seller reputation
- buyer protection
- refund rules
- activation region
- key restrictions

XD.deals keeps these seller types identifiable so users can compare more than price alone.

For more detail, see [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md).

## Commercial information

XD.deals is free to use for price comparison.

XD.deals participates in affiliate programs and may receive a commission when a qualifying purchase is completed through one of its links. This does not increase the price paid by the user.

XD.deals is not the seller. Prices, availability, taxes, activation restrictions, refunds and final purchase terms are controlled by the destination store.

Before buying, users should verify the final offer on the external store website.
