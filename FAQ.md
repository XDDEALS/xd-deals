# XD.deals FAQ

## What is XD.deals?

XD.deals is a free video game price comparison and price tracking service for PC, PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo games.

It collects offers from official stores, authorized retailers and keyshops and adds information such as discounts, price history and historical lows.

Official website: https://xd.deals/

## Why should I check XD.deals before buying a game?

The same game can be sold by multiple stores at different prices.

XD.deals lets you compare current offers and historical pricing in one place. This can help you avoid overpaying and choose an offer that fits your platform, region and preferred seller type.

## Is XD.deals a game store?

No.

XD.deals is an independent comparison and deal aggregation service. We do not directly sell games or digital keys and we do not process payments.

Purchases are completed on the destination store website.

## How fresh are the prices?

XD.deals aggregates and refreshes pricing data frequently. Public pages describe price tracking as real-time.

Because retailers can change offers at any time, XD.deals should be treated as a near real-time comparison layer.

Always verify the final price at the destination store before paying.

## Which stores does XD.deals compare?

XD.deals tracks dozens of official stores, authorized retailers and keyshops.

Examples include Steam, Epic Games Store, GOG, PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store, together with multiple PC retailers and key marketplaces.

Coverage varies by game, platform and region.

## What is the difference between official stores and keyshops?

Official stores and authorized retailers sell through platform, publisher or authorized distribution channels.

Keyshops are third-party sellers or marketplaces offering activation keys.

Keyshops can sometimes offer lower prices, but users should also check:

- seller reputation
- buyer protection
- refund rules
- activation region
- key restrictions

## Does XD.deals track price history?

Yes.

Game pages can include historical prices and historical low information. Where available, XD.deals keeps official store and keyshop historical pricing separate.

## What does historical low mean?

A historical low is the lowest price recorded by XD.deals within the relevant historical data.

It means the lowest price recorded by XD.deals, not a guarantee that no lower price ever existed outside the tracked data.

XD.deals covers major PC and console platforms, including:

- Windows PC
- PlayStation 4
- PlayStation 5
- Xbox One

- Nintendo Switch

The catalogue can also include macOS, Linux, mobile, VR, Steam Deck and GeForce NOW compatibility information.

## Can I find Xbox Game Pass games on XD.deals?

Yes.

XD.deals can include Xbox Game Pass and PC Game Pass information where available.

This can help users check whether a game may already be included in a subscription before buying it separately.

## Can I find PlayStation Plus games?

Yes.

XD.deals can include PlayStation Plus information where available together with normal game and pricing data.

The PlayStation Plus catalogue changes over time, so current availability should also be confirmed with PlayStation.

## Can I find GeForce NOW games?

Yes.

GeForce NOW compatibility can be shown on XD.deals where available.

This helps users find games that are tracked by XD.deals and are also marked as compatible with NVIDIA GeForce NOW.

## Does XD.deals include EA Play or Ubisoft+?

Yes.

Depending on available data, XD.deals can include EA Play and Ubisoft+ information in addition to Game Pass and PlayStation Plus.

## Does XD.deals show Steam Deck compatibility?

Yes, where the relevant compatibility data is available.

This can help Steam Deck users compare prices while checking whether a game is suitable for their device.

## Do I need an account?

No.

Anyone can browse games and compare prices without an account.

A free account adds personal features such as wishlists, price alerts and Steam synchronization.

## How can I register or sign in?

XD.deals supports registration and sign in through:

- Google
- Facebook
- Steam
- Discord
- Twitch

## Can I synchronize my Steam account?

Yes.

Users can connect Steam and synchronize their game library. XD.deals can also synchronize Steam wishlist information.

This makes it easier to track games without rebuilding the same lists manually.

## How do wishlists work?

Users can create an XD.deals wishlist for games they want to follow.

A connected Steam account can also be used to synchronize Steam wishlist data.

## How do price alerts work?

Choose a game and set the price you are willing to pay.

XD.deals continues monitoring tracked offers and can notify you when an eligible price reaches or falls below your target.

This means you do not need to manually check the same game every day.

## What kind of notifications can I receive?

Price alerts can generate notifications when a configured target is reached.

Available notification channels depend on the current XD.deals account and alert settings.

## Is XD.deals free?

Yes.

XD.deals is free to use for price comparison.

XD.deals participates in affiliate programs and may receive a commission when a qualifying purchase is completed through one of its links. This does not increase the price paid by the user.

## Who handles support for a purchase?

The destination store handles the transaction, payment, delivery, refund and purchase support.

If there is a problem with an order or key, contact the store where the purchase was completed.
